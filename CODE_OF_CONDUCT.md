# Working Together

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss the work, support disagreements with
evidence, and leave room for questions and correction. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Raise conduct concerns privately with the repository maintainers. Greater Goods
employees should also use the company's established reporting process. Keep
personal information out of public issues and pull requests. Maintainers may
moderate contributions that disrupt constructive collaboration.
